Initial Laurel has a great way of listening, and synthesizing what makes a website unique as a medium to design for. Continually in my practice I'm interested in this mode of finding common ground that begins with listening to and noticing what makes the thing unique compared to another. Web is not print, it's not an identity, it's not necessarily an app either. For one, a website is durational: its existence is dependent on a constant flow of energy into servers that host it. In other words, it's "live". Because of this it's dynamic, it's a site of constant flux. I love the example Laurel uses the talking about the early days of The Creative Independent where the conversations and new knowledge that's learned shapes the form of the website. This ad hoc approach of having listening and responding built into the process is very interesting to me.
