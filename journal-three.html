Initial Unfortunately the state of the internet is a result of its greatest benefit: the freedoms it allows. So when discussing the topic of an increasingly user-hostile web, I believe it's a matter of considering what it really means to be free. Do less rules mean more opportunity to do what you want? Or does it only provide the space for other more insidious entities to fill the void. Without institutions that can compare to these corporations we continue to be subject to their ability to corner and divide attentions.
